Privacy policy

Last updated 20 November 2026. This policy covers Kiban's private beta. It will be reviewed by a lawyer before Kiban is offered to the public, and we will tell you in the app before any material change.

Kiban is run by Simon Sam in Melbourne, Australia. Questions or requests: hello.kiban@gmail.com.

1. What Kiban collects and why

  • Your account: your email address and sign-in (through our sign-in provider, or Sign in with Apple), so you can sign in and only your household sees your information.
  • What you add: tasks, notes, family profiles (names, relationship, birthday, school or class, activities, preferences), events, house rules and settings. Children do not have accounts; a parent or guardian adds their details.
  • Chats: what you type or paste into a chat with Kiban and its replies, saved to your account until you delete them.
  • Photos: only a photo you choose to send (for example a school note). It is read once and not stored on Kiban's servers.
  • Usage records: which feature ran and how much AI it used (never the text), to keep costs and daily limits in check.

2. Google data (only if you connect Google)

Google shows you the exact permissions before you agree. Kiban asks to read your Gmail (gmail.readonly), see and change your calendar events (calendar.events) and list your calendars. Only if you ask Kiban to save a reply draft does it ask separately to create drafts (gmail.compose); you send drafts yourself.

  • Email: Kiban reads email from the senders you tick (and, only if you switch on "Read all my email", recent email from other senders). Its code turns each email into a short fact: sender, kind, date, amount, a subject line and a link. It does not keep the email text. Facts are deleted after 60 days, when you untick the sender, when you disconnect Google, or when you delete your account. Kiban never sends, changes or deletes email.
  • Card briefs: if you allow AI, the text of an email that became a card is sent to our AI provider once to write a short summary of the card (what to do, the time, what to bring, one link from the email). Kiban keeps only that summary, for up to 60 days.
  • Chat questions about your email: if you allow AI and ask, Kiban searches your Gmail read-only and sends the matching emails to the AI for that one answer. The email text is not stored.
  • Calendar: Kiban reads your events live to show your week and does not keep a copy. It adds, changes or deletes an event only after you confirm. During the beta, automatic adding of events from email is switched on only for the operator's own test accounts.
  • Tokens: Google access tokens are stored encrypted, are never sent to your phone, and are used only by Kiban's servers acting for you.
Google API Services User Data Policy

Kiban's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Kiban does not use Google user data for advertising, does not sell it, and does not use it to train general-purpose AI models. People at Kiban do not read your email unless you ask for help with a specific problem and agree, or the law requires it.

3. Who else processes your data

  • Supabase stores the database and runs Kiban's servers, in Sydney, Australia.
  • Anthropic (the Claude AI service), only if you switch AI on. It receives the text a feature needs (for example the email behind a card, your chat, or a photo you send). Anthropic states that it does not train its models on this data and deletes it within 30 days by default.
  • Google receives the requests Kiban makes to your Gmail and Calendar for you.
  • Apple provides Sign in with Apple, Face ID and speech-to-text on your phone.
  • Open-Meteo receives your suburb and time zone for the weather.

We do not sell your information and the app has no advertising, analytics or tracking software. Some of these services may process data outside Australia.

4. Your choices

  • Switch AI off at any time in Settings. Kiban then sends nothing to the AI and the rest keeps working.
  • Disconnect Google (Settings, Account & data): Kiban asks Google to cancel its access and deletes the tokens and everything it read from your email.
  • Export your data as one file, or delete your account, which deletes your sign-in and your household's data immediately.
  • You can also revoke Kiban's access from your Google Account at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • If you can't use the app, email hello.kiban@gmail.com and we will act within 30 days.

5. Security

Each household can reach only its own data, Google tokens are encrypted, AI keys stay on the server, and your AI permission is checked on the server for every AI request. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects you, we will tell you.
