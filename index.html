The school emails you don't have time to read, turned into what needs you.

Kiban is an iPhone app for busy parents. Connect your Google account and Kiban reads the emails from the senders you choose (school, sport, swimming, after-school care, bookings), works out the dates, deadlines and things to bring, and shows them as short cards and a calendar you can trust.

What Kiban does
  • Turns school and activity emails into cards: what to do, when, what to bring, and a button to the right page.
  • Shows your week from Google Calendar, and adds events when you confirm.
  • Keeps simple family profiles so cards can say whose event it is.
  • An evening note of what's on tomorrow.
  • A chat that can answer "when is the swimming carnival?" from your own email.
Your data

Kiban never sends, changes or deletes your email. It reads only what you allow, keeps short facts rather than your emails, and you can disconnect Google or delete your account in the app at any time. AI features are off until you switch them on. Read the privacy policy.

Kiban is in a small private beta with invited households. It is not yet on the App Store.
